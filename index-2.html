01 · The problem and the person

A plan at 6. Nothing to wear.

For Dublin women aged 18–35 getting ready at short notice. Segment and location are pilot assumptions.

Invented customer quote
“I have the invite. I don’t have time to shop around.”
Illustrative wording.

People currently search several sites, borrow clothes or rush to a shop. None answers one urgent question: can my size arrive before I leave?
