# Pronto submission checklist

Use this to finish the parts that need your own GitHub account or a real person's input.

## Publish

- [ ] Create a public GitHub repository and enable Pages from `main` / root.
- [ ] Copy the live `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/pronto-fashion/` link.
- [ ] Open it in a private window and on a phone and laptop.
- [ ] Capture screenshots of the full landing page and demo for the Moodle submission if the upload point accepts a document.

## Check the alternatives at source

- [ ] Open [Zara Ireland](https://www.zara.com/ie/). Write down the current Irish delivery option relevant to the problem and the date you checked it.
- [ ] Open [REVOLVE](https://www.revolve.com/). Write down the current Irish delivery option relevant to the problem and the date you checked it.
- [ ] Update the page only with facts the official pages support. The concept currently avoids claiming that either retailer cannot provide fast delivery.

## Ask someone else to test

Give the tester your public `github.io` link and ask them to do these tasks on their phone:

1. Find a women's going-out look in size 10 under €150.
2. Reserve it and refresh the page.
3. Clear the saved sample reservation.

Record their actual feedback here before adding it to the public page:

- Tester (first name or “friend”; do not publish contact details):
- What they tried:
- What worked:
- What confused them or failed:
- Change made after the test:

Do not fill this section with a made-up test result.

## Write your own reflection

The brief requires 150–300 words in your own words, with no AI or paraphrasing tool. Use your own experience to answer:

- What did you learn about using technology to fit a real customer's situation?
- What specific part of building Pronto did AI make possible or faster?
- What was difficult, including the test or publishing step?
- What did the outside tester say, and what did you change?
- What would you do differently next time?

Do not paste AI-written prose into this section.

## Moodle

- [ ] Follow the submission point's instructions. The brief conflicts: page 2 requests a link plus reflection in the text box; page 4 requests a Word/PDF with two GitHub Pages links, full-page screenshots and reflection.
- [ ] Submit the actual GitHub Pages link (not the `github.com` repository URL).
- [ ] Include your independently written reflection.
- [ ] Keep the public repository unchanged until marks are released.
